# Final paper

`final_paper.pdf` is the completed report; `final_paper.tex` is its editable
source. Tables are generated from verified CSVs by
`python3 scripts/build_paper_tables.py`; figures are the retained vector PDFs
in `results/analysis/` and the original bandwidth cohort. Compile the TeX
source with Tectonic or an appropriate LaTeX distribution. Proposal and final
paper are separate: the proposal remains a two-page planned-work document.

The final paper reports actual results, negative outcomes, provenance gaps,
aggregate miss-attribution limits, and checker/cache-history effects. It does
not claim physical cache-port area/energy costs or general runtime-input
correctness. `SHA256SUMS` fingerprints all paper files except itself.
